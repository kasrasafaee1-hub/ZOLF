Questions before you apply

Do I need to already be running ads?

No — the first money back comes from clients already in your database, not from new ad spend. Paid traffic, if it's used at all, comes later once the fundamentals are fixed.

How fast will I actually see money back?

Depends on your starting point. Businesses with an existing list and reputation typically see money back by Week 2–4. Brand new businesses are slower — usually Week 6–8. We tell you which bucket you're in before you commit to anything.

What if I don't have a client list?

Then this probably isn't a fit right now — there's no fast payback available without an existing list or a budget to acquire one. We'd rather tell you that up front than take a call that goes nowhere.

Is this a marketing agency?

No. We diagnose whichever of sales, marketing, team, or systems is actually capping your revenue, fix that first, then run a 6-month plan covering all four — not just content or ads.

Why do I have to apply instead of just booking?

The math has to work before a call is worth either of our time — client list size and average ticket need to clear a bar. The short form tells us that before we get on a call.
